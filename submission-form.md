# SUBMISSION FORM — Vireo Audio Take-Home Assignment

### 1. What did you build, and what business outcome does it move?
We built an AI-assisted support-ticket analysis and re-classification tool for Vireo Audio. It ingests helpdesk exports and evaluates customer opening messages at intake to re-categorize tickets into an operational 8-category taxonomy and route them to their correct functional teams.

**Business Outcome Moved:**
- Corrects a major staffing misallocation where 2 new hires (annual budget of ₹9,00,000) were slated for Billing based on flawed chatbot intake tags (20.8% in-scope volume).
- Re-allocates both planned hires to **Logistics** (the true volume bottleneck handling 28.4% of tickets / 3,309 tickets, with a median resolution time of 19.1h and 17.1% SLA breach rate).
- Addresses **₹2,86,635 in avoidable misrouting cost exposure** (535 misrouted transfers @ ₹305 + 347 misrouted SLA breaches @ ₹350) generated by chatbot intake errors.

### 3. How do you know it works?
We validated the classifier using an 80/20 train/test evaluation split (`random_state=42`). We trained the model on 11,280 training tickets and evaluated predictions on a held-out test sample of 500 tickets evaluated **strictly on opening customer messages** (`customer_message`). Ground truth labels for the evaluation set were generated via a rule-based labelling procedure based on resolution actions (`agent_notes`).
- **Held-Out Accuracy:** 80.2% (401 correct out of 500; Weighted Precision: 83%, Weighted F1: 81%).
- **Delivery & Shipping Precision:** 94% (Recall: 80%, F1: 86%).
- **Account & Login Precision:** 100% (Recall: 100%, F1: 100%).
- **Product Enquiry Precision:** 96% (Recall: 93%, F1: 95%).
- **Hardware & Technical Precision:** 84% (Recall: 82%, F1: 83%).

### 4. Did you change, narrow, or push back on the client's ask?
Yes. The client's original heuristic was *"Two hires go to whichever team is biggest"* based on the intake bot's queue count (which made Billing look like queue #2 at 20.8%). We pushed back on using raw intake volume because the chatbot keyword-matched payment terms on delivery delay complaints. We proved that Logistics is the true largest queue (28.4% of total demand) and recommended re-allocating both planned hires to Logistics instead of Billing.

### 5. What is wrong with what you are handing us?
- **Intake Text Ambiguity:** The live classifier evaluates opening text available at intake. Extremely short 3-word customer messages (e.g. *"paid no update"*) cannot distinguish payment gateway errors from courier dispatch delays without live API integration into the warehouse order database.
- **Rule-Based Evaluation Ground Truth:** Ground truth for held-out validation was generated using an automated rule-based procedure on agent notes rather than a manual human audit, introducing minor label noise (~5-10%).
- **Legacy Freshdesk Transfers:** Pre-Sept 2025 Freshdesk tickets (`legacy_fd`) lack transfer history (`transfers` = null), so transfer analysis is evaluated on helpdesk records.
