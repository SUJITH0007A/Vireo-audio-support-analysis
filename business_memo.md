# MEMORANDUM

**TO:** Priya Raman, Head of Customer Experience, Vireo Audio

**SUBJECT:** Headcount Allocation & Support Queue Analysis (Jan 2025 – Jun 2026)  

---

### Executive Finding
The planned allocation of two new support hires (annual budget of ₹9,00,000) to the **Billing** team is based on misleading intake data. While the automated intake chatbot tags **20.8%** of tickets as Billing, **over 40% of these tickets are actually delivery delay and shipment issues** misrouted because customers mentioned payment terms (*"paid"*, *"money deducted"*). The true operational bottleneck is **Logistics**, which handles **28.4% of all customer tickets (3,309 tickets)** and experiences the highest service level breach rate across the company.

### Current Problem
1. **Intake Chatbot Misrouting:** The intake bot automatically routes any query containing payment keywords to Billing. In practice, customers write *"paid 10 days ago but package not delivered"*, forcing Billing agents to read, re-handle, and transfer these tickets to Logistics.
2. **Operational Strain in Logistics:** Under current intake routing, Logistics tickets take a median of **25.1 hours** to resolve. Under re-classified actual workload demand, Logistics is handling 3,309 tickets with an **SLA breach rate of 17.1%** (highest in the company) and 685 internal hand-offs.
3. **Billing Over-staffing Illusion:** Genuine payment and invoice queries represent only **11.6%** of total demand (1,351 tickets) and are resolved in a median of **0.5 hours**.

### Recommended Staffing Allocation
**REALLOCATE BOTH PLANNED HIRES (2 HEADCOUNT) TO LOGISTICS.** Zero hires should go to Billing.

### Financial & Resource Implications
1. **Planned Staffing Allocation:** The **₹9,00,000 / year headcount budget** (2 hires @ ₹4.5L/year) is directed away from an over-staffed Billing queue to the true volume bottleneck in Logistics.
2. **Current Operational Cost Exposure:** Support operations experienced **₹4,52,200** in total SLA breach store credit penalties (1,292 breaches @ ₹350) and **₹3,96,195** in direct transfer re-handling costs (1,299 hand-offs @ ₹305) during the in-scope period.
3. **Avoidable Misrouting Exposure:** Correcting chatbot intake misrouting eliminates up to **₹2,86,635** in avoidable re-handling overhead and SLA penalties generated by misrouted Billing tickets (996 misrouted tickets generating 535 transfers and 347 SLA breaches).

### Validation Confidence
The AI classification model was evaluated using an 80/20 train/test split with a fixed random seed (`random_state=42`). Evaluated on 500 held-out test tickets using **only opening customer messages**, the classifier achieved an overall accuracy of **80.2%** (weighted precision: 83%, weighted F1: 81%), with **94% precision** on Delivery & Shipping queries.

### One Important Limitation
Intake classification evaluates customer opening messages. Extremely brief 3-word customer messages (e.g. *"paid no update"*) remain ambiguous at intake without live API integration into the warehouse order status database.
